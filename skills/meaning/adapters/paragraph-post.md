# Adapter: paragraph and post

## Paragraph

Do not hunt for "grand philosophy" in every paragraph. A paragraph performs a local function:
introduce a thought - provide evidence - turn the argument - complicate the thesis - link two parts - give an emotional pause.

Paragraph report (AUDIT) / paragraph brief (TRANSLATE):

```
Function of the paragraph:
What it claims:
What the claim rests on:
What the reader must already know by this point:
Where the meaning jump is (if any):
What can be cut:
```

In AUDIT, after the report -- up to 2 fix options per problem (step-7 rule from SKILL.md).

## Post

Checks (AUDIT) / render requirements (TRANSLATE):

- Is there a **single thesis** (= the map's `claim`)? One post, one thesis.
- Does the thesis **develop**, or just repeat itself in other words?
- What work does **each block** do? A block that does not move the thesis forward is a candidate for removal.
- **Opening and close:** does the opening promise one thing while the close delivers another?
- Are concepts introduced **before** the text relies on them?
- Is there a real **shift in the reader's view** (= `audience_shift`): what will they start noticing after reading?

For TRANSLATE of a post: the render gives a skeleton (thesis -> blocks -> what each block does), then recommend a narrative-strategy pass (a narrative map) and a voice pass (if this is a channel post). Do not polish the final style here.
