## Episode as a document (SCP mechanic)

The SCP Foundation works without a single centralized canon: articles can contradict each other, and "canons" are clusters of related works that share assumptions. The format itself is the worldbuilding engine: a recurring field report, trial protocol, or creature-care instruction gives every episode immediate structure while gradually revealing the institution/world behind the scenes.

Durable format techniques:

- A strong shared document format.
- An institution/frame able to encounter almost any anomaly.
- Formal language in contrast with strange content.
- Redaction/omission of information.
- Reusable terminology.
- Multiple access levels.
- Built-in disagreement (different versions/sources).
- Local continuity without requiring global uniformity.

## Conflict and story generators from the bible

Sources for new episodes WITHOUT inventing new lore (antidote to lore inflation, see `00-creative-constitution.md`):

- **Sanderson's Third Law -- expand what already exists rather than adding new.** One world element can be explored through medicine, crime, grief, education, advertising, class privilege, war, religion, romance -- this produces a more coherent universe than a new superpower every episode.
- **Question generator for faction/group cards (if any):** what would make this group split or collapse? This question turns static lore into a story engine.
- **Engagement loop (5 steps):** a legible incident -> an unresolved residue -> pattern recognition (a link to a past episode) -> audience interpretation (comments) -> authorial response (the next episode confirms/complicates/reverses).
- **Epistemic reward of every arc:** a confirmed relationship, a revised timeline, an uncovered cost, an exposed liar, a symbol made clear -- a mystery with no periodic payoff reads as annoying, not intriguing.

## Local vs. global coherence

Every episode holds two structures at once:

- **Local legibility:** a new viewer understands the want/obstacle/turn without context.
- **Global resonance:** a returning viewer recognizes continuity, symbols, relationships, consequences.
